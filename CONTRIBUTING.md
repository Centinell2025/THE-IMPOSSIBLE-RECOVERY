# Contribution and Evidence Quality Standards

All additions must remain fictional, original, reproducible, and defensively oriented.

- Keep the eight-server architecture and atmospheric-emergency storyline coherent.
- Do not reuse other creators' storylines, artifacts, flags, or questions.
- Do not invent claims of real compromise or real meteorological measurements.
- Prefer deterministic generators and validate SHA-256 manifests.
- Every question must be solvable from included artifacts.
- Preserve ambiguity only where the correct response explicitly recognizes uncertainty.
- Never commit instructor-only answer keys or private walkthroughs.
- Keep challenge materials in English.
