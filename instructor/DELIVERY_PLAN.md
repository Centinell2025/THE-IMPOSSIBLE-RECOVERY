# Advanced Instructor Delivery Plan (Public-Safe)

## Intended audience
Experienced SOC analysts, incident responders, digital forensic examiners, and infrastructure reliability engineers.

## Training format
- Pre-brief: 30 minutes — objectives, evidence provenance, continuity policy
- Investigation: 120–180 minutes — independent artifact analysis
- Review: 45 minutes — hypothesis defense and UTC timeline reconciliation
- Debrief: 30 minutes — lessons learned, operational implications, and uncertainty

## Assessment dimensions
- Timeline accuracy and clock normalization
- Cross-source corroboration
- Backup content-integrity reasoning
- Audit-policy compliance analysis
- Evidence citation quality
- Clear distinction between facts and speculation

## Student deliverables
- Evidence inventory with hashes
- Normalized incident timeline
- Audit compliance assessment
- Recovery-point recommendation
- Executive summary with confidence and limitations

## Instructor restrictions
This is a public-safe plan. Do not upload the confidential walkthrough, expected responses, answer key, or hidden grading material to this public repository. Maintain them in a separately access-controlled workspace.

## Before official submission
Conduct independent blind solves; fix ambiguous prompts; check file sizes and artifact formats; prepare platform-specific grading and walkthrough in private; confirm HTB's current content requirements.
